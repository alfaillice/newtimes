## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit.

## First-party cookies

NewTimes.news does not set any cookies of its own: we have no user accounts and no analytics tools. Fonts and the site's other resources are served from our own server.

## Third-party cookies: Google advertising

The site is funded by **Google AdSense** advertising (Google Ireland Ltd.). Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- show adverts, measure how often they are seen and prevent fraud;
- if you consent, show personalised adverts based on your interests.

On your first visit you will see a consent window managed by Google, where you can accept, reject or choose by purpose. If you reject, non-personalised adverts may still be shown; these only use what is strictly necessary to work and to measure fraud.

You can change your choice at any time with the **"Cookie settings"** link at the foot of every page.

More information on how Google uses data: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage advert personalisation in [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Live video

The [Live](/en/live/) page embeds a live broadcast using YouTube's "privacy-enhanced" player (youtube-nocookie.com). When you play the video, YouTube may store data in your browser in accordance with its privacy policy.

## How to delete or block cookies

As well as the consent window, you can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block all third-party cookies, the site will keep working normally.
