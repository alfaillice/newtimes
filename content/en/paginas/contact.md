---
Write to us at **{{ cfg.titular.email }}**. You can write in English or Spanish.

So that we can help you quickly, say in the subject line what it is about:

- **Correction:** you have spotted a mistake in a story. Include the link and which fact is wrong.
- **Rights:** you believe some content infringes your copyright or image rights.
- **Privacy:** you want to exercise your rights over your personal data.
- **Suggestion:** anything else.

We answer every message, usually within 48 working hours.
