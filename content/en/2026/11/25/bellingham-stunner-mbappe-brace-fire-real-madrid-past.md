Real Madrid came from behind to beat PSV Eindhoven 5-1 at the Santiago Bernabéu in Madrid on Tuesday, with Jude Bellingham scoring a spectacular volley and Kylian Mbappé netting twice. The win was Madrid's fifth from five European matches this season and their 10th in a row across all competitions, and it moved them provisionally to the top of the Champions League league phase table.

PSV, the Dutch champions, took the lead from a penalty. Aurélien Tchouaméni was judged to have trodden on Sven Mijnans's foot, and Ivan Perišić scored from the spot. PSV had been the more competitive side in the first half, with Ruben van Bommel drilling a shot wide early on, while Vinicius Junior struck the bar at the other end.

Madrid's coach, Jose Mourinho, the Portuguese manager, left Bellingham on the bench for the first half, explaining that the England midfielder had a minor fitness issue. At half-time, with his side behind, Mourinho made three changes, bringing Bellingham on among them, and Madrid were transformed.

Five minutes into the second half, Federico Valverde levelled with a finish from a quick team move. Vinicius crossed for Yan Diomande, whose pass fed Arda Güler; the Turkish midfielder's back-heel set Valverde up to score at the near post. Madrid kept pressing, and after goalkeeper Matej Kovar saved from Güler and Mbappé, Bellingham made it 2-1. He collected a loose ball on the edge of the box, flicked it up and volleyed it across Kovar and inside the far post.

Mbappé added the third after being set up by Güler, then rounded Kovar for the fourth. Vinicius, who had not scored for Madrid since August, wrapped up the win after Bellingham played a pass in behind PSV's defence. Mourinho celebrated the goal, hoping it would mark an overdue return to form for the Brazilian forward.

Madrid's Belgian goalkeeper, Thibaut Courtois, was critical of the first half even as he praised the response. Speaking in Spanish, which is translated here, he said: "I think our first half was not good again. In modern football, if we don't all run, it's impossible." He also admitted that he had changed his dive at the last moment on the penalty, having overthought it. He said the half-time talk, in which Mourinho showed the team footage of what was going wrong, had helped change the team's attitude, and that the early goal and the second one made the game easier.

Madrid's performance in the second half, after a difficult first 45 minutes, gave Mourinho's side a result that moved them clear at the top of the provisional standings.
