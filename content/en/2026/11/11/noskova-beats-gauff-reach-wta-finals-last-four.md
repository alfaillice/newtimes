Linda Noskova beat Coco Gauff 6-3 6-3 to reach the semi-finals of the WTA Finals, according to BBC Sport, which describes her as the first player to reach the last four of the tournament. The Czech player, who won her first Grand Slam title at Wimbledon in July, played with what the broadcaster called controlled aggression and served very well throughout.

BBC Sport reports that Noskova, ranked eighth in the world, broke Gauff's serve four times in the first set alone. She sent powerful winners to all corners of the court against an opponent the broadcaster considers one of the best movers on the tour. In the second set she made only one unforced error. The 21-year-old finished the match with an ace, a fitting end, BBC Sport said, to her debut at the tournament.

Gauff, who is fourth in the rankings, had a poor day on serve, according to the report. She hit eight aces and eight double faults, and won only six of 24 points behind her second serve. She had won a close match against her compatriot Jessica Pegula on Sunday.

Speaking afterwards, Noskova said: "I feel like I stayed in the match through every moment, from beginning to end." The win was her sixth against a top-10 opponent this season, BBC Sport noted.

The result leaves the group unsettled. Gauff and Pegula can still qualify for the last four, the broadcaster said. Earlier in group one, Pegula beat Ukraine's Marta Kostyuk 6-2 7-5, a match that BBC Sport said had seven breaks of serve and that was her first win since September. Kostyuk had been dealing with a wrist injury since August and was only told she would play on Saturday, after world number one Elena Rybakina withdrew. She had lost her opening match to Noskova on Sunday.

BBC Sport is the sole source for these details. The report does not say how the group standings will be decided if Gauff and Pegula finish level.
