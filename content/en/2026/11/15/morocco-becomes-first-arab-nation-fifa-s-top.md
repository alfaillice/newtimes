Morocco have become the first Arab country to enter the top five of FIFA's men's national team rankings, according to Al Jazeera, which reported the move on Sunday. The Atlas Lions climbed two places from their previous best position of seventh and moved ahead of Brazil and Portugal on points.

Al Jazeera described Morocco as a two-time World Cup quarterfinalist. The network said the side are also the first African and Arab team to reach back-to-back World Cup quarterfinals, and that both times they were knocked out by France. Its report noted that Morocco's 2022 semifinal run in Qatar, when they became the first African and Arab team to reach that stage, remains their best World Cup showing, and that the 2026 campaign in North America did not match it.

The outlet also said Morocco are the only Arab country, and the first African nation since Nigeria in 1994, to be ranked fifth in the world. Morocco are one of the co-host nations for the 2030 World Cup.

In the current round of qualifiers for the Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) 2027, Mohamed Ouhabi's team has won all of its matches, according to Al Jazeera. On Friday it beat Niger 5-0 in Rabat, in front of a home crowd.

Morocco's latest title, however, is disputed. Al Jazeera reported that Senegal beat the hosts 1-0 after extra time in January's AFCON final in Rabat. Senegal had walked off the pitch for 14 minutes after a late penalty was awarded against them, and several players returned to the dressing room in protest before the match resumed. Morocco missed the spot kick, and Senegal scored the winner in extra time.

The Confederation of African Football (CAF) initially declared Morocco champions. Two months later, CAF's Appeal Board ruled that Senegal had forfeited the match by leaving the pitch, overturning the result and awarding Morocco a 3-0 win. Al Jazeera said Senegal have appealed to the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS), which will hold a hearing on the matter. The outlet described the title as Morocco's second AFCON crown since 1976.

According to Al Jazeera, the ranking gain comes after a strong run in continental football, although the title dispute is not yet resolved.
