Saudi-led coalition forces said they had intercepted two ballistic missiles and two drones aimed at Riyadh, the Saudi capital, according to Al Jazeera. The report places the interceptions within a wider escalation of the war in Yemen, where the coalition backs the internationally recognised government against the Houthis.

The coalition's spokesperson, Turki al-Maliki, said in a post on X early on Sunday that air defences had destroyed the two missiles, which he said had been launched by the Houthis towards Riyadh. Earlier on Saturday, he had said that two suspected Houthi drones were intercepted and destroyed over Riyadh and in the southern regions of the kingdom. Al Jazeera reports that the launches are alleged to have been carried out by the Houthis.

The Houthis are an armed movement that controls Sanaa, Yemen's capital, and that Al Jazeera describes as an Iran-allied rebel group. According to the Saudi authorities, as cited by Al Jazeera, at least 26 people have been killed in suspected Houthi drone and missile attacks in Saudi Arabia since 23 September. The Houthis have repeatedly targeted King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, as well as the East-West pipeline and oil infrastructure in eastern and southern Saudi Arabia, the broadcaster reports.

Al Jazeera also reports that fighting has intensified along several front lines in Yemen. Colonel Majid al-Nuzaili, spokesman for the Yemeni army, said government forces had made gains during heavy fighting in the al-Hazm and Madghal districts of al-Jawf governorate. He also said that 321 Houthi leaders had been killed or wounded in 723 targeted operations in Taiz, Lahij, Marib and al-Jawf governorates, and that 117 military vehicles had been destroyed. These are his figures, as reported by Al Jazeera. The Houthi military spokesman, Yahya Saree, said the coalition had carried out 72 air strikes against the Houthis in Saada, Taiz, Lahij and al-Jawf over the previous 24 hours, and that the group had fired ballistic missiles at Saudi-backed forces in Kharaz, in Lahij. Al Jazeera separately reports that the coalition carried out at least 37 air strikes, 56 drone attacks and 541 artillery strikes.

Media outlets affiliated with the Houthis reported that two women and two children were injured by artillery shelling by Saudi-backed forces in a residential area of the Shuaub district of Sanaa. Al Jazeera does not say when the shelling took place.

The International Organization for Migration (IOM), a UN agency, says more than 340,000 people have been displaced by fighting in Yemen since August, according to Al Jazeera.

The US Embassy in Riyadh issued a nationwide security alert on Saturday, urging Americans in the kingdom to exercise "heightened vigilance" because of an increased threat of Houthi attacks. The embassy said attacks on civilian airports and other critical infrastructure in Saudi Arabia had caused civilian casualties and serious travel disruption, and warned that hostilities could spread rapidly. "Please take extreme caution if you choose to use airports in Saudi Arabia," it said.

All of the information in this story comes from a single outlet, Al Jazeera, and the claims about launches, casualties and military operations are attributed to the parties who made them.
