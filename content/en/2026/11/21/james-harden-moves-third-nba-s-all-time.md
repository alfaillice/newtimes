James Harden scored 51 points to move third on the NBA's all-time list of 50-point games, passing Kobe Bryant, according to BBC Sport. The 37-year-old's performance helped the Cleveland Cavaliers beat the Indiana Pacers 128-119 in the NBA Cup.

Harden made all 14 of his free throws and also recorded six rebounds and six assists at Rocket Arena in Cleveland. BBC Sport said it was his 26th game with 50 or more points, and his first since he scored 55 for the Los Angeles Clippers in November 2025.

The record remains with Wilt Chamberlain, who has 118 50-point games, the broadcaster noted. Michael Jordan is second with 31. At 37 years and 86 days, Harden is the fourth oldest player to score 50 points in a game, BBC Sport said.

BBC Sport described Harden as emotional after the match. He said that being part of the same conversation as Chamberlain, Jordan and Bryant was special, saying "it's very humbling and it's definitely special". Harden, the ninth highest scorer in NBA history with 29,633 points, said he is in his 18th season and still wants to be impactful. He said the game had given him confidence that he could still play at a high level, while acknowledging that he is not as fast as he was when he was 23 or 24.

The Cavaliers travel to the Chicago Bulls in the NBA on Tuesday, BBC Sport reported.
