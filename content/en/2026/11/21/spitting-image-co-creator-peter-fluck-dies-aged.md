Fluck was born in Cambridge in 1941. He created the show with his childhood friend Roger Law, whom he had met while studying at the Cambridge School of Art. After leaving school, the two moved to London to work as freelance illustrators. In 1976 they set up their partnership, “Luck and Flaw”, back in Cambridge.

ITV eventually axed the series because of declining audience numbers. Fluck then moved to Cornwall, where he turned to ceramics, printmaking and mobiles. In 2018, Law donated his archive of the show to Cambridge University. The collection, which includes original scripts, puppet moulds, drawings and recordings, is held in the university library.
