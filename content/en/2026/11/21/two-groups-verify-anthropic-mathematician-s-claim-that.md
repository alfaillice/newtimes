The Riemann hypothesis was proposed in 1859 by the German mathematician Bernhard Riemann. It concerns prime numbers, which are whole numbers greater than 1 that can be divided only by themselves and 1, and how they are distributed along the number line. The Clay Mathematics Institute has named it one of seven Millennium Prize problems and offers $1m for each one solved.

Alpöge's note appeared a day after Anthropic went public on the Nasdaq stock market. He acknowledged the timing was poor and said he would have finished the work earlier if he could.
