Marcus Henley, manager of Hudson Valley Foie Gras in Ferndale, New York, told the local outlet Radio Catskill that the fire destroyed two buildings housing ducks and geese. "The building isn't there anymore," he said. He suspected an electrical malfunction may have started the blaze, although this has not been confirmed.
