The strait is one of the world's most important oil routes. Before the current conflict, it carried nearly a fifth of the world's crude oil supplies. The incidents take place in a war involving Iran, the United States and Israel, which began in late February when the United States and Israel attacked Iran. Tehran has since moved to tighten its control over the waterway.

According to Serdar, Iranian forces have warned that ships which do not comply will either be attacked or hit sea mines laid by the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) or other Iranian forces. He said Tehran was deliberately escalating tensions to signal that if Iran cannot export its oil, other countries will not be able to do so either, and to show that it still controls the strait.
