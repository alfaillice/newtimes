Other disputes concern electricity and carbon capture. The draft proposes raising electricity's share of the world's final energy consumption to 35 per cent by 2035, but does not say the power must come from renewables. Some countries want the word "clean" included. Others reject expanding carbon capture, a technology that traps emissions from power plants and factories, arguing it is costly, unproven and could allow polluters to keep burning oil and gas. Vanuatu's climate minister, Ralph Regenvanu, wants references to what he called false solutions removed.
