There are concerns, the paper reports, that several challengers could strengthen Infantino, who may present himself as the candidate of unity after months of infighting. Concacaf, the confederation for North and Central America and the Caribbean, has been reluctant to endorse an alternative candidate despite opposing Infantino. Some of its federations argue that a boycott would send the strongest message about the need for governance reform.
