Ineos, the petrochemical company of Sir Jim Ratcliffe, has reached an out-of-court settlement with Sir Ben Ainslie over the £180m yacht the two sides' companies built for the 2024 America's Cup, according to The Guardian. The paper reports that the settlement ends a legal action Ineos launched in March. In a statement, the company said it had "reached a settlement with Athena Racing and considers the matter now closed". It gave no further details, including whether the boat has been handed back.

Court documents filed earlier this year, also cited by The Guardian, showed that Ineos was seeking more than £10m over what it described as Athena's refusal to return the boat. The case had been due for trial in January 2028 in the admiralty division of the courts of England and Wales. Those claims have now been withdrawn.

The dispute ends a relationship that had produced a notable success. Ratcliffe and Ainslie worked closely across two America's Cup cycles, and in 2024 Ineos Britannia became the first British crew to win the challenger series. The two fell out over plans for a third campaign. Last year Ineos withdrew its challenge for the 2027 event in Naples, and it later expressed surprise that Ainslie's team, Athena Racing, had chosen to keep using Britannia for its own 2027 bid. Britannia had been beaten 7-2 by Emirates Team New Zealand in the 2024 final.

The two sides have given very different accounts. Ineos said that delays in agreeing terms with Athena had undermined its ability to prepare, and that it had spent £180m on Britannia on top of £170m on the boat used in the 2021 campaign. Athena has argued that it always owned the assets and intellectual property from both campaigns, and that Ineos was a sponsor. A spokesperson for Athena Racing/GB1 told The Guardian that the team "ha[s] always been, and remain[s], the owners of the assets, including the AC75 race yacht".

In a defence filing in June, Athena alleged that Rob Nevin, chair of Ineos Sport, had warned Ainslie on the eve of the 2024 event that Ratcliffe would "burn his house down" if he did not give the billionaire what he wanted. The Guardian reports these claims as allegations made in the filing.

Michael Grade, chair of the Royal Yacht Squadron, Ainslie's club, told members in a statement that the dispute had been settled and that all claims had been withdrawn. He said the team was preparing to challenge for the 38th America's Cup in Naples next year, according to The Guardian.

Ineos's record in sport has been mixed since Ratcliffe began investing in 2019. Since buying Team Sky, The Guardian notes, Team Ineos has added only the 2019 Tour de France and the 2021 Giro d'Italia. Its sponsorship of the All Blacks ended in a dispute with New Zealand Rugby, which was settled out of court, and it also settled with Tottenham after a sponsorship deal was terminated early. Its current football and sporting interests include Nice, which has been for sale for more than a year, and Manchester United, where Ineos holds a 29% stake. Manchester United are in the Premier League's relegation zone and last week appointed Mauricio Pochettino as their third permanent manager in less than three years.
