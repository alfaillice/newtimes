Andy Robertson will equal Kenny Dalglish's record for Scotland appearances when the national side hosts North Macedonia on Friday, according to The Guardian. Dalglish ended his international career with 102 games, a total that has stood for four decades. Robertson, the Tottenham full-back, will reach the same figure in that match and should overtake it when Scotland play Switzerland on Monday.

The Guardian reports that Robertson was keen to stress that matching the figure does not place him on the same level as Dalglish, who is widely regarded as the finest player the country has produced. “He’s definitely the greatest from Scotland,” Robertson said, pointing to what Dalglish achieved in his career and to the kind of person he has been throughout his life.

Robertson spent nine years at Liverpool, where he got to know Dalglish well, and the newspaper says that connection gives the story a personal dimension. According to The Guardian, Dalglish often reminds Robertson that reaching 100 caps was much harder in his time than it is now.

Robertson told the newspaper that he has never set himself personal goals and that his focus has always been on the team. He recalled that after he overtook Darren Fletcher's total, it felt like a long wait to reach Jim Leighton's figure of 91. Once he was one game away from Dalglish, he said, he started to believe the milestone could happen and began teasing Dalglish that it would.

Robertson also said he would stand alongside Dalglish in terms of the number, but that he did not want people to forget how important Dalglish had been to Scotland. He said that if the milestone is reached, he would be very proud of it.

Scotland host North Macedonia on Friday and face Switzerland on Monday. The Guardian's report does not include the scores or the current group standings.
