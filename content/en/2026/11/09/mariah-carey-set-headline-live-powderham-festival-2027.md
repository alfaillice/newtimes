Mariah Carey is set to headline a music festival in 2027, according to the BBC, which reports that the organisers of Live at Powderham have announced the singer as the event's third headliner.

Organisers said Carey will bring her Firefly summer tour to Powderham Castle, in Devon, near Exeter, on 27 June 2027. She will be joined by special guests Sugababes, while the R&B group Eternal will provide additional support, the BBC said.

The festival's other headliners are Shania Twain, who has been set to perform on 24 June 2027, and Alex Warren, who is due to perform on 26 June, the broadcaster reported.

The BBC describes Carey as the best-selling female solo artist and the singer of All I Want For Christmas Is You and Love Takes Time. It says she has amassed more than 19 Billboard Hot 100 Number One singles, more than any solo artist in history.

Her recent performances include appearing at the opening ceremony of the 2026 Winter Olympics in Italy, the BBC notes.

Live at Powderham attracted more than 140,000 people over its four days in 2026, according to the broadcaster. That year's headline performances came from Lewis Capaldi, Alanis Morissette and Teddy Swims.

The BBC's report relies on the organisers' announcement. It is the only outlet cited for these details, so the lineup and dates should be checked against the festival's own statements as they become available.
