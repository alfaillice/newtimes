Eight people have been sentenced to prison over a mass brawl at a hotel in Abu Dhabi on Wednesday that involved mixed martial arts (MMA) fighters Usman Nurmagomedov and Merab Dvalishvili, BBC Sport reported.

According to BBC Sport, a video of the incident appeared to show Nurmagomedov, who is Russian, slapping Dvalishvili, a Georgian and former UFC bantamweight champion, after approaching him from behind. Fighting then broke out between the two men and members of their teams. Abu Dhabi police later confirmed that the incident led to ten arrests.

BBC Sport said a social media post from Abu Dhabi's judicial department stated that all ten defendants had been convicted for their part in the brawl. Citing a report by The National, an Abu Dhabi news outlet, BBC Sport said six defendants were sentenced to six months in prison, fined £62,000 each and ordered to be deported after completing their sentences. The convictions were for 'disturbing public security, causing panic and endangering lives'.

Two more defendants received the same prison term, according to the same report, but for violating privacy by using information technology to film and publish footage of the brawl. The remaining two were fined £10,200 each for disturbing public security and assaulting others. None of the defendants has been named.

BBC Sport reported that Chad Bronstein, co-founder of Real American Freestyle (RAF), said Dvalishvili had been released from custody. 'I'm pleased to share that Merab Dvalishvili is back with us in Abu Dhabi,' Bronstein said in a statement. BBC Sport said it had asked the judicial department to confirm the outcome.

Dvalishvili, 35, was scheduled to fight Brazilian Marlon Moraes at an RAF event in the United Arab Emirates on Saturday. Nurmagomedov, 28, was in Abu Dhabi to support his brother Umar, who was due to face American Urijah Faber on the same card. After the incident, Dvalishvili posted a video in which he called Nurmagomedov and his team snakes while receiving treatment on his bloodied face.

BBC Sport noted that the two teams had clashed earlier at an RAF event in July. UFC champion Islam Makhachev, a close friend and training partner of the Nurmagomedov brothers, suggested that this earlier altercation had led to Wednesday's brawl.

Nurmagomedov is the PFL lightweight champion but is out of contract after his last bout with the organisation in July. He has been widely linked with a move to the UFC, but on Friday the promotion's president, Dana White, said he had not spoken to the Russian. According to BBC Sport, White also called the altercation 'ridiculous' and 'stupid', and described those involved as 'a bunch of idiots'.
