Spain's football federation has banned FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono for 12 matches over the challenge that led to the death of Tenerife striker Jesús de Miguel, according to The Guardian. The federation's disciplinary committee imposed the maximum sanction on Friday 13 November, the British newspaper reported.

Owono, an Equatorial Guinea international, was sent off in a second division match on 2 November. The Guardian reported that he raced into midfield to clear an aerial ball and collided with De Miguel, who was 30. The striker was placed in intensive care in hospital and died on 3 November.

In its statement, the committee said its assessment had to be based on the referee's report, which said Owono was sent off for “violently striking an opponent in the chest with his leg, using excessive force, while the ball was in play”. According to The Guardian, the committee said the death was public knowledge and that, given the conduct and the surrounding circumstances, the maximum sanction was justified.

The Guardian reported that Spanish police sources had said an investigation into what happened had been opened under judicial supervision. The newspaper did not give further details of that inquiry.

In a statement published by his club, Owono said these were “the hardest days of my life”. According to The Guardian, he wrote that he felt immense pain over the loss of De Miguel and that he would face whatever came next “with the utmost respect”. FC Andorra head coach Carles Manso said on Thursday 12 November that it would be Owono who decides if and when he returns to football.

Tenerife fans and players held a memorial and farewell for De Miguel at the club's stadium on Saturday 7 November. His funeral took place on 5 November, and The Guardian said around 400 mourners attended, including Spain head coach Luis de la Fuente and Real Madrid president Florentino Pérez.
