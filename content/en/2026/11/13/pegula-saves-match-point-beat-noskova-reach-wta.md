Jessica Pegula saved a match point to beat Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova 7-6 (7-2), 4-6, 7-5 and reach the last four of the WTA Finals, the season-ending event of the Women's Tennis Association (WTA), according to BBC Sport.

The result knocked out Coco Gauff. BBC Sport reported that Gauff had beaten Marta Kostyuk earlier on Thursday, which meant Pegula needed to win against Noskova to advance. The match was a fluctuating contest, and BBC Sport said Pegula showed strong mental resilience to take it.

Noskova had already secured her place in the semi-finals before the match, and she will face either Elina Svitolina or Aryna Sabalenka on Saturday. BBC Sport said the Czech player had gone a break up in the third set and held a match point on Pegula's serve at 5-4, but could not convert it.

According to BBC Sport, Pegula served 16 aces, the most she has produced in a WTA Tour match. She also returned well to wrong-foot Noskova. The Czech player was below her best on her own serve, hitting 15 double faults against nine aces.

Pegula will now face French Open champion Mirra Andreeva for the first time, BBC Sport said. The two have not met before, and the report did not give any further detail on their head-to-head record.

The earlier match between Gauff and Kostyuk was also a test of endurance. BBC Sport reported that Gauff received treatment on her lower back midway through the match and at one point trailed 5-1 in the second set. She then won six games in a row to complete a 6-4, 7-5 victory. Kostyuk, a late call-up to the finals after Elena Rybakina withdrew, was also dealing with a wrist problem that has affected her since August, according to the same report.

Gauff said after the match: "Physically, I wasn't at my best, which I'm not really used to." BBC Sport said she had also come back from 5-1 down to beat Pegula on Sunday, the second time she had overturned that deficit at this tournament. The report said Gauff struggled with her serve, with nine double faults and one ace, and that she found it painful to complete her service motion.

All of the details in this story come from a single report by BBC Sport. Other outlets had not been checked at the time of writing.
