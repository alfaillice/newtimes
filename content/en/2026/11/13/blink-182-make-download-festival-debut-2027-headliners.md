Blink-182 will make their debut at Download festival next year as one of its headliners, the BBC has reported. The California pop-punk band are on the bill alongside Evanescence and Bring Me The Horizon for the 2027 edition, which will take place at Donington Park in Leicestershire, England, from 9 to 13 June.

According to the BBC, the three headliners are among more than 100 rock and metal acts booked for the event. Other names include The Prodigy, 5 Seconds of Summer and a special performance by heavy metal veterans Mötley Crüe. Turnstile, Faith No More, Body Count featuring Ice-T and Malevolence have also been confirmed, and the festival has said more acts will be announced.

The BBC notes that Blink-182 emerged from the skate punk scene in California in the 1990s. The band have had two UK top 10 singles and had their first number one album in 2016, 21 years after their first record. Next year will be their first appearance at Download.

The broadcaster also reports that Evanescence will become the festival’s second female-fronted headliner, after Linkin Park topped the bill in 2025. Bring Me The Horizon, the Sheffield-formed band, are returning to headline for the second time in five years. They have announced that they will re-record their 2006 debut album, Count Your Blessings, to mark its 20th anniversary. Frontman Oli Sykes told the BBC Radio 1 Rock Show that going back to their roots was “a lot of fun”.

Download describes itself as a festival spanning rock, metal, punk, emo, hardcore, alternative and classic rock. The organisers called this year’s line-up “bigger, better and bolder than ever”, the BBC reports, and said it offers something for everyone.

Not every fan was convinced. The BBC reports that some people who commented on the festival’s Instagram post questioned whether a headliner such as Blink-182 suited a metal festival. Others said the appeal of Download lies less in the line-up than in its community and atmosphere. One fan who is hoping to attend her ninth Download in a row told BBC Newsbeat that she was happy with the bill but that the weekend’s energy mattered more to her.

Pre-sale tickets are already available. General sale opens at 10:00 GMT on Sunday, according to the BBC.
