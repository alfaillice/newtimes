Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, a 15-year-old opener, scored 204 from 53 balls on Tuesday as India A beat the United Arab Emirates (UAE) by seven wickets in the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament in Doha. Chasing 250, India A reached 254 for three in 14.2 overs, guaranteeing their place in the semi-finals.

Sooryavanshi hit 28 sixes and seven fours. The sixes total is the most ever recorded in a senior men's innings, passing the 26 struck by Tanmay Agarwal for Hyderabad against Arunachal Pradesh in India's first-class Ranji Trophy in January 2024. It also surpasses the List A record of 23, set by D'Arcy Short for Queensland in 2018.

He also broke several speed records in List A, the category for senior one-day matches. He reached his century in 28 balls, one ball quicker than Australia's Jake Fraser-McGurk managed in October 2023. He brought up 150 in 40 balls, improving his own mark of 59 from December 2025, and reached 200 in 52 balls, roughly half the 103 balls Chad Bowes needed in October 2024. He was caught at long-off with his side five runs from victory.

The UAE, who chose to bat, made 249 for eight in their 50 overs. The side was not their first choice and included several players with international experience. Sooryavanshi put on 68 for the first wicket with Abhishek Sharma, who scored 12, then 40 with Prabhsimran Singh, and 137 with Rinku Singh, who finished unbeaten on 27.

India A captain Tilak Varma described the innings after the match: "No words to say for him. I would only say it's like absolute carnage." Varma added that the match was effectively over after five overs, when the side had scored 100.

Sooryavanshi made his Indian Premier League debut in 2025 at the age of 14 for Rajasthan Royals. This year he scored 776 runs in the competition, the most of any batter, at a strike rate of 237.30. He made his Twenty20 International debut for India against England in Manchester in July, becoming the country's youngest men's international. He has won 10 T20 caps but has yet to play 50-over cricket for India.

Former India batter Deep Dasgupta praised his technique and natural power, saying in radio comments that "he's definitely the real deal."

India A top Group B after a win over Nepal and a defeat by Pakistan A. Pakistan A can move back to the top if they beat Nepal on Tuesday. The semi-finals are scheduled for Thursday and Friday, with the final on Sunday.
