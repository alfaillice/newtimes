Persistent rain and very strong wind gusts are forecast for much of Galicia, the north-western region of Spain, on Sunday as an Atlantic weather front crosses the country. According to Europa Press, which relays the forecast of Spain's State Meteorological Agency (Aemet), the western half of the region will see rain that could be heavy and, locally, accompanied by thunderstorms.

Winds from the west and south-west could gust up to 80 kilometres per hour in Galicia, as well as in the Cantabrian mountain range and, at some points, in the interior of Cantabria and the upper Ebro valley. Strong gusts are also expected along the coasts of Galicia and the Cantabrian Sea.

Galicia is the only region with active weather warnings. According to the forecast, a yellow warning for rain, wind and swell will remain in force until 17.00.

Elsewhere, skies will be mostly cloudy or overcast across much of the country. The rain linked to the front will affect the north-west of the Iberian Peninsula, although some showers are also possible in the Betic ranges of the south. Clearer skies are expected in the Mediterranean regions. Snow is forecast on mountains above 2,000 metres. Mist and fog are likely over wide areas of the Atlantic side and the north-east, and could persist locally in the mountains. The largest rainfall totals are expected in western Galicia, in mountainous areas of the north and in the west of the Sistema Central, the mountain range that runs through central Spain.

Temperatures will generally change little, but the forecast points to a mixed picture. Daytime highs will fall slightly across most of the peninsula, more markedly in the Ebro valley, and will not change in the Balearic or Canary Islands. Overnight lows will drop across the whole country, especially in the south and east of the peninsula, with weak frosts possible in high areas of the Pyrenees. Among the provincial capitals, the highest daytime temperatures are forecast in Alicante and Murcia, at 24 degrees Celsius, and the lowest in Burgos, at 12 degrees. The coldest night is expected in Ávila, at 5 degrees.

Winds from the west and south-west will blow across the peninsula and the Balearic Islands, moderate in the interior and the north, with strong gusts along the coasts of Galicia and the Cantabrian Sea. In the Canary Islands, the trade winds will be light or moderate. The Balearics will have mostly clear skies, with a light drizzle possible at the end of the day, while the Canaries will see some cloud and isolated showers on the mountainous islands.
