Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has become the youngest captain in top-level men's Twenty20 cricket, according to BBC Sport, after taking charge of his state side Bihar on Saturday. Aged 15 years and 232 days, he broke the record previously held by Zach Vukusic, who captained Croatia at 17 years and 311 days.

Bihar's first match in the season was not a success. They lost their opening game in the Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy, India's domestic state T20 competition, which sits below the Indian Premier League (IPL). Saurashtra beat them by five wickets, and Sooryavanshi scored 18 from 12 balls.

The captaincy is the latest landmark in a fast-moving career. Sooryavanshi became India's youngest men's international in July, when he made his T20 debut against England. He had already made his IPL debut for Rajasthan Royals as a 14-year-old in 2025, becoming the youngest player in the franchise tournament. Later that season he became the youngest centurion in men's T20 cricket, hitting a hundred against Gujarat Titans. This year he was the tournament's leading run-scorer.

Earlier this month he played in two of India's five T20 internationals in New Zealand, with a top score of 45 in the third match, in Wellington. He is also due to be part of India's side at the Rising Stars Asia Cup, a 50-over development tournament taking place in Doha later this month.
