The Spanish Socialist Workers' Party (PSOE) has cancelled a campaign event planned for Monday 16 November in Madrid, where Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez and Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva were due to appear together. According to Europa Press, the cancellation is due to a loss of voice affecting the Brazilian president.

Sánchez, who leads Spain's government, is also the Socialist candidate for re-election in the general election on 29 November. Government sources had said last Thursday that, alongside an official meeting at La Moncloa, the prime minister's office and residence complex in Madrid, a second event would likely take place in which Lula would express his support for Sánchez ahead of the vote.

The Moncloa sources quoted by Europa Press said the official visit remains scheduled for Monday. The two leaders are expected to discuss bilateral relations and coordination on global initiatives, including Democracia Siempre and the Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty.

Lula's trip is his first visit to Spain since he won Brazil's presidential election on 25 October, defeating Flávio Bolsonaro. He is making a stop in Madrid on his return from the COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, and the Asean summit in Manila, the Philippines.

The voice problem had already affected his international tour, according to Europa Press. It cites the Brazilian newspaper Folha de S.Paulo, which reported that Lula could speak on Saturday but was not in a condition to take part in long public appearances. Folha also reported that he cancelled his remarks during his official visit to the Philippines, where he gave no speeches after meetings and instead issued written statements.

The visit follows the 30th Ibero-American Summit held in Madrid, which Lula did not attend because of exhaustion from the campaign in Brazil. Sánchez has cited Brazil in pre-campaign rallies to encourage a Socialist victory, although polls predict a negative result for his party, Europa Press reports.

The source does not say whether the rally will be rescheduled.
