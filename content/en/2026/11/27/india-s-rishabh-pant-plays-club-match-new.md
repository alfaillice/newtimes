India's wicketkeeper-batsman Rishabh Pant played a social club match in Christchurch on Wednesday under the name 'Stan Stan', the day before India's second Test against New Zealand.

Pant, 29, turned out for the Open House Swingers in a Last Man Stands match against The Chosen Ones at Hagley Park. The ground should not be confused with neighbouring Hagley Oval, where the Test starts on Friday local time. The scorecard listed him as Stan Stan. He made 54 from 21 balls, and the Open House Swingers won by 16 runs.

Mohammad Safi, a player for the Open House Swingers, said the appearance was a chance encounter. He saw Pant riding an electric bike in central Christchurch, stopped him and asked whether he would play. Pant agreed, put on pads and hit several sixes. Safi said the opposition did not recognise Pant while he wore a bright tracksuit, and that at one point Pant asked him for a single before asking for a turn to show how to hit the ball.

India's bowling coach, Morne Morkel, the former South Africa fast bowler, said he 'nearly fell off my chair' when he saw footage of the innings circulating on social media. New Zealand captain Tom Latham called Pant's performance 'brilliant' and said it was good that he was giving his experience back to local Last Man Stands teams.

The innings came during a busy spell for Pant. In the first Test in Wellington, he scored 128 from 153 balls as India posted 720-9 in a match that ended in a draw. The total was a record for India in New Zealand. Pant, who plays for Lucknow Super Giants in the Indian Premier League, has scored 3,853 runs in 53 Tests at an average just short of 45. Among Indian batters with more than 2,000 Test runs, only Virender Sehwag and Kapil Dev have a better strike rate than his 74.43.

The second Test between New Zealand and India begins on Friday at Hagley Oval.
