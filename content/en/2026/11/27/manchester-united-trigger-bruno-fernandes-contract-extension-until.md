Manchester United have activated a one-year option in the contract of captain Bruno Fernandes, keeping the Portugal midfielder at the club until the summer of 2028. His deal was due to expire in June. By exercising the option, the club have removed the risk that he could agree a pre-contract with a club abroad from January and leave on a free transfer next summer.

Sources say both the player and the club judged that activating the option was in their best interests. Because the contract now runs on, overseas clubs will not be able to approach Fernandes when the transfer window opens on 1 January. If he leaves in the summer, United would also be in a position to demand a fee for him.

The longer-term picture is less settled. Fernandes said earlier this month that talks over a further extension had been put on hold "because of the context of the team and the club". Sources say those discussions will resume once the team's league position improves.

The immediate focus is on results. United are second-bottom of the Premier League, England's top flight, with one win. Their start under head coach Mauricio Pochettino has been difficult: they lost 4-0 at Liverpool in his first league game, then drew 2-2 at Sporting in the Champions League.

At his news conference on Friday, Pochettino was asked about his approach and said: "I am not nice." He added that the team needed to be strong and to build something, rather than simply be pleasant.

Fernandes is a doubt for Saturday's home match against Brentford after missing training on Friday with a virus. Pochettino said he would decide on the captain's fitness on Saturday morning, adding: "We are not going to take any risks."

Pochettino also confirmed that midfielder Carlos Baleba is expected to be out until late January with a hamstring injury. Marcus Rashford, who has been out since 13 October with a hip problem, has begun individual work as he works towards a return.
