Morgan Gibbs-White scored twice as Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea 3-2 at the City Ground on Friday night in the Premier League, England's top flight. Igor Jesus gave the home side the lead inside the first five minutes with a first-time volley into the top corner, and Emmanuel Emegha twice pulled a goal back for the visitors.

The result is Forest's second successive home win and lifts them three points clear of the relegation zone. For Chelsea, it was a fourth defeat in five league matches, and the match ended with the head coach, Xabi Alonso, facing questions about his team selection.

The decision that drew most attention before kick-off was the choice of Wesley Fofana, the France defender, to start at centre-back alongside Moisés Caicedo. Fofana had been the subject of criticism during the week after comments he made in a French interview about missing a penalty in an EFL Cup defeat at Liverpool. Chelsea supporters at the ground made their feelings known, and Fofana was booed.

His night got worse early on. He was caught out by a long channel ball from James McAtee, allowing Liam Delap to run clear, and then fouled the former Chelsea striker in the box. Gibbs-White converted the resulting penalty. Fofana was booked and was replaced by Levi Colwill shortly before the hour.

Chelsea came back into the game. Pedro Neto's shot was deflected off Jair Cunha and fell for Emegha, who had come on at half-time for Danny Welbeck, to score from close range. Later, Forest's Xaver Schlager played a pass that Gibbs-White ran onto to make it 3-1. Emegha then headed in at the far post to bring Chelsea back to 3-2, but the visitors could not find an equaliser.

Alonso defended his decision to keep Fofana in the side. "We need to support everyone," he said after the match, adding that his team had to go through difficult moments and needed to stick together.

Forest's head coach, Oliver Glasner, was asked about the closing minutes, when his side allowed Chelsea back into the match. "I wouldn't say it was frustration," he said. "But I would say it was unnecessary because we did really well. Unnecessary but we will learn from it. If you win, it's OK."

The result leaves Forest in a stronger position in the table, while Chelsea, who have now lost four of their last five league games, face questions about their form and their defensive selections.
