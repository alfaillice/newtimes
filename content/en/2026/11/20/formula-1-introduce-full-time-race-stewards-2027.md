Formula 1's governing body, the FIA, will introduce permanent race stewards from the 2027 season, according to BBC Sport. The broadcaster reports that a small number of full-time stewards will be recruited during 2027, with the group expanded from 2028, in line with an FIA statement.

The move responds to repeated calls from grand prix drivers. BBC Sport says drivers have complained in briefings with FIA officials that rulings on driver standards and racing errors were not applied consistently, and that penalties for on-track incidents have been criticised for the same reason.

Under the current system, a small number of stewards are rotated around races. Each grand prix has a group of four stewards, one of whom is a former driver. BBC Sport notes that the change is therefore not a major departure from the present arrangement. The FIA said that driver stewards, usually former F1 drivers, would continue to serve.

In its statement, the FIA said full-time stewards would bring continuity and expertise to the stewarding structure, and that they would allow "greater consistency in decision-making". Existing stewards will remain part of a mixed roster of full-time and part-time officials for the foreseeable future, the federation said.

The announcement follows the opening of an FIA race operations centre at F1's Biggin Hill headquarters in south London. BBC Sport reports that the FIA has in recent years received higher income from F1, which provides the majority of its funding, on the understanding that this money would go towards improving officiating and rule-making.

FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem described the recruitment as "a significant step in our longstanding mission" of building a dedicated officiating system in the championship. He said it was also an investment in the future of the sport.

BBC Sport also recalls that last year Williams driver Carlos Sainz, a director of the Grand Prix Drivers' Association, said that F1 and the FIA agreed that permanent stewards were the way forward, with at least two of the three stewards on each race being permanent. The FIA has not yet set out how many full-time stewards will be recruited beyond the initial small group.
