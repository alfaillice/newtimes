Poland says a Russian cruise missile came down on its territory early on Friday, during one of the largest Russian air attacks on Ukraine since the full-scale invasion began in 2022. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the incident did not appear to be an accident, as it occurred while Western military exercises were under way in the region. No injuries or damage have been reported.

The two outlets differ on how many objects crashed. The BBC reports that Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said a Kh-101 cruise missile hit a rural area at Nowy Lipowiec, about 50km (31 miles) from the Ukrainian border, at about 06.30 CET. He said a second suspected missile fell near Rudnik nad Sanem and a jet-powered drone crashed near Mołodycz. The Guardian, citing reports, says two cruise missiles and a jet-powered drone came down, and that Kosiniak-Kamysz confirmed three violations of Polish airspace.

The exercise is called Koalicja 2026. It is led by the United Kingdom and France and involves troops from 11 countries in the Coalition of the Willing, a group of European and allied states preparing for a possible peace deal in Ukraine. The drills are taking place near Nowa Dęba, between 30 and 60 miles from the sites where the objects fell, according to the Guardian. Tusk described the incident as “an intentional provocation” by Russia. He said he had spoken with Mark Rutte, secretary general of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO), who agreed that the timing did not seem a coincidence.

Rutte condemned what he called “grave violations of Polish airspace” and said NATO jets had been scrambled in response. He is due to visit Warsaw on Saturday. The UK Defence Secretary, Wes Streeting, who is in Poland for the exercises, described the incursion as “very serious”, according to the Guardian. Kosiniak-Kamysz said he would ask NATO for additional support, the BBC reports. Tusk summoned the heads of the army and security services to an emergency meeting. Air traffic control at Rzeszów-Jasionka airport, an international passenger hub and a major transit point for military aid to Ukraine, was briefly evacuated overnight, the first time this has happened since the full-scale war began.

In Ukraine, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the attack was “one of the largest” since 2022. He said at least eight people had been killed and nearly 30 injured, according to the Guardian. The BBC reports that at least eight people were killed on Friday as missiles and drones struck three bridges over the River Dnipro in Kyiv and a station in Lviv. Maj Gen Ireneusz Nowak, commander of Poland's operational command, said Russia had used seven bombers, 26 Kalibr and 72 Kh-101 cruise missiles and more than 500 drones. The BBC says Russia has made no comment on the violations of Polish airspace.

Poland has experienced similar incidents before. In July a Russian Kh-101 cruise missile fell in the Lublin region, leaving a crater 10m (33ft) wide. In September 2025, more than 20 Russian drones entered Polish airspace, and Tusk said the country was closer to military conflict than at any time since the Second World War. Deaths in Poland have been rare. In November 2022, two Polish farmers were killed by a missile that was believed to be a Ukrainian air-defence missile fired in response to a Russian attack.
