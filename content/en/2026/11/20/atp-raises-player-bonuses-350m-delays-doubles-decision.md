The doubles proposals have faced fierce opposition from players. They include halving draw sizes and cutting the share of prize money going to doubles from 20% to 10%. In October, more than 150 men's doubles players wrote an open letter urging the ATP to reconsider what they called “drastic” changes. Doubles players have said the changes would make it “effectively impossible” for anyone outside the top 30 to make a living from the sport.
