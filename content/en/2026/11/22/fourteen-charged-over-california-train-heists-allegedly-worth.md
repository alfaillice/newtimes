Fourteen people in southern California have been charged with stealing more than $22m worth of goods from moving freight trains, according to The Guardian. The case, which the paper compares to the outlaw era of the American West, has been brought by the US justice department, the federal body that prosecutes crimes at national level. Prosecutors say the group raided at least 36 trains and sold thousands of stolen Nike trainers, along with other products, on the black market.

Bill Essayli, first assistant US attorney, told the Guardian: "The scale of the theft is staggering." The alleged ringleader, Nathan Daniel Angel, is 21. The scheme, which the group called "Los Trenes" ("the trains"), ran from 2024 through 2026. Court documents did not list a lawyer for Angel or the other people charged.

According to a grand jury indictment, Angel's associates, including his father and two uncles, hid on board moving freight trains. Some acted as lookouts while others slashed the train's air hose. This caused a sudden drop in air pressure and forced an emergency stop. The Guardian notes that the manoeuvre is dangerous because of the risk of derailment. Once a train had stopped, the rest of the crew loaded the goods and hid them in houses across southern California, prosecutors say.

The Guardian reports that the evidence includes a February 2024 photograph, attached to court documents, showing Angel posing in front of boxes of Nike merchandise. Video evidence shows Donta Hedgeman, 27, sawing through the lock of a container on a moving Nike train in October 2024.

The thefts grew in 2025, even as there were signs that police were closing in. On 17 April 2025, an unidentified "co-conspirator" texted Carlos Luis Fernando Martinez, 23, to say they had been arrested for receiving stolen property. Twelve days later, the crew took 1,170 tools and 17,000 parts from Milwaukee Tool. Police also appeared to be looking into an unnamed company that had bought a large number of stolen products from the crew in autumn 2025.

On 16 January 2026, Angel and Fredy Marquina, 25, discussed a partner whom police had intercepted. The men carried out further heists in the summer of 2026. In July 2026, Angel Sanchez, 24, shared an AI-generated image that appeared to glamorise the robberies. It showed masked men throwing Nike trainers from a Burlington Northern Santa Fe Railway (BNSR) train into luxury cars marked "Los Trenes".

If convicted of the conspiracy charge, the men face at least five years in prison. The charge of theft from interstate railroad carries a maximum of 10 years. A conviction for violence against railroad carriers could bring the most severe penalty, which includes the possibility of life imprisonment. The charges are allegations, and none of the defendants has been convicted.

Prosecutors described the indictment as the end of a 14-month investigation. Nathan Hochman, the Los Angeles county district attorney, said: "We are determined to protect this vital supply chain and ensure that goods reach their intended destinations, not the black market."
