Miley Cyrus has married her partner of four years, Maxx Morando, in a ceremony in Palm Springs, California, the BBC reports. Photographs of the wedding, which took place on Saturday, show the couple standing under an arch covered in white flowers beside a swimming pool.

According to the BBC, which cites the US entertainment programme Entertainment Tonight, the singer's mother, Tish Cyrus, and her sister, Noah Cyrus, attended. The BBC says Cyrus wore a white dress for the occasion.

The Grammy-winning singer, whose hits include “Flowers” and “Party in the U.S.A”, had already confirmed their engagement in an interview with Good Morning America, a US breakfast television show. The BBC reports that she said Morando had dropped to one knee to propose during a trip to Asia. She described being surprised, saying she was “so, so, so surprised”.

Speaking to Wonderland magazine, Morando said he “had to be really sneaky” to keep the plans secret. He told the magazine the couple went first to Tokyo for a few days and then to Kyoto, and that he waited until the final day of the trip to propose, so that Cyrus would not suspect what was coming.

The BBC notes that Cyrus, 33, was previously married to the Australian actor Liam Hemsworth. The pair married in 2018 after about a decade together and separated in 2019. Cyrus told British Vogue in 2023 that she and Morando, who is 28, had met on a blind date.

Morando is a drummer with Liily, a band based in Los Angeles. According to the BBC, the group appeared in 2022 on NBC's Miley's New Year's Eve Party, which Cyrus hosted, alongside musicians including her late godmother, Dolly Parton, and David Byrne.

The BBC report does not include a statement from the couple about the ceremony itself.
