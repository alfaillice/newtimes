Under his leadership, the business moved into luxury markets and expanded beyond jewellery into watches, perfumes, leather goods and hotels. The brand's celebrity clients have included Elizabeth Taylor, Audrey Hepburn, Marilyn Monroe, Ingrid Bergman, Sophia Loren, Anne Hathaway, Zendaya and Rihanna.

The funeral will take place in Rome on Monday, 23 November, at the Basilica of Santa Croce in Gerusalemme.
