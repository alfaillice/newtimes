Kansas City Current won the National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) championship for the first time on Saturday, beating the Washington Spirit 2-1 at Audi Field in Washington, DC, according to Al Jazeera. It was a third consecutive final defeat for the Spirit, who had also lost the championship match in 2024 and 2025.

According to Al Jazeera, Vanessa DiBernardo gave Kansas City the lead early in the second half. Her in-swinging corner looped into the net after Kayla Sharples blocked the Spirit goalkeeper, Sandy MacIver. Debinha, the Brazilian international, added the second goal deep in stoppage time, finishing a sweeping counterattack.

Washington hit back in the 98th minute, when Trinity Rodman headed in a goal, but there was no time left for an equaliser. Al Jazeera reports that Rodman, a 24-year-old United States star, had been caught in a physical aerial challenge from Kansas City’s Laney Rouse after five minutes. She needed treatment on the sidelines and returned to the field grimacing and holding her shoulder, but she played on until the end. She was in tears at the final whistle.

DiBernardo was named the championship’s most valuable player. Al Jazeera notes that she had returned to professional football only months earlier, after the birth of her daughter. Speaking after the match, she said: “We knew it was going to be a battle; we felt good coming out of the first half.”

The title is a first for Kansas City, which had lost the championship game to the Portland Thorns in 2022. For Washington, the defeat was another painful end to a season that had brought them back to the final, as Al Jazeera’s report makes clear.

Al Jazeera is the only outlet from which this account has been drawn, and the details above are as it reported them.
