A-Lim Kim had started the third round with a three-shot advantage. On Saturday, at the Tiburon Golf Club in Florida, the player returned a 69 with six birdies and three bogeys, two of which came on the last three holes. That card left A-Lim Kim on 18 under overall.

The biggest threat comes from world number one Nelly Korda of the United States. Before the third round, Korda was six shots off the lead. Korda then shot a 65, seven under par, with nine birdies and two bogeys, to move to 16 under. That leaves Korda two shots behind A-Lim Kim going into Sunday.

England's Charley Hull is on 14 under after a 69 on Saturday, with four birdies and a bogey. Hull is among the chasers but sits further back than the leading group.

The final round will decide the title and the $4m top prize. The leaderboard shows a close race at the top, with three players within one shot of each other and Korda in pursuit.
