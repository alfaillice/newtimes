Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya will miss Tuesday’s Champions League match at home to Borussia Dortmund, the German club, because of a shoulder injury. Manager Mikel Arteta said the problem does not appear to be serious and that surgery will not be needed.

Raya was forced off in the second half of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw at Newcastle on Saturday. He hurt his left shoulder while claiming a cross and making contact with Newcastle forward William Osula. Initially, the injury was feared to be a dislocated shoulder, which raised concern that the goalkeeper could be sidelined for a long time.

Raya had a scan after Arsenal returned to London and did not take part in training on Monday. Speaking about the Dortmund game, Arteta said: “He’s not fit for tomorrow, unfortunately, but it doesn’t look too serious.” He added that the pain Raya showed straight after the incident had worried the squad, but that the situation appeared better than expected.

Arteta said further tests would be carried out on Tuesday to establish the next steps. Asked whether Raya could play against Manchester City at home on Sunday, he said the club would know more after those tests.

Kepa Arrizabalaga is expected to take over in goal against Dortmund. Arteta said Arsenal were well covered in that position, pointing to Kepa and third-choice goalkeeper Illan Meslier, and stressed that the squad needed to be prepared for injuries of this kind.

Dortmund are a point behind Arsenal in the Champions League, Europe’s leading club competition, after three wins from their first four matches. In the Bundesliga, Germany’s top division, Dortmund lead Bayern Munich by two points, although the game is expected to be a test for a youthful Arsenal side. Arteta said the squad would include Jobe Bellingham, who was called up by England for the first time during the most recent international break, alongside his brother Jude.

Arsenal’s next fixtures after Dortmund are against Manchester City, Brentford, Tottenham Hotspur and Real Madrid.
