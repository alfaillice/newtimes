Liverpool beat Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday, in a match that turned in the eighth minute when Leny Yoro was sent off. It was the first game in charge for Mauricio Pochettino, the Argentinian who took over at United earlier in the week, and the result left the club second from bottom of the Premier League, England's top division.

The decisive moment came after Ronald Araújo played a long ball forward for Alexander Isak. Isak took a heavy first touch, and Yoro, who had been slow to react, clattered into the Sweden international just outside the area. The referee, Sam Barrott, showed Yoro a straight red card. Dominik Szoboszlai curled the free kick into the bottom corner from 19 yards, and United's wall did not cover the spot where the ball was placed.

Liverpool kept up the pressure. Rio Ngumoha, the teenager, was given a clear run at United's right side, and Florian Wirtz finished a volley after the cross. Ngumoha became the youngest scorer in the fixture's Premier League history when he scored in the 66th minute, after a pass from Bradley Barcola. Víctor Muñoz added the fourth with a finish after Wirtz had released him inside the box.

Pochettino said after the match that the dismissal was wrong and that United should have been awarded a penalty moments earlier, for a challenge by Jérémy Jacquet on Bryan Mbeumo. Bruno Fernandes, United's captain, said the decision needed to be looked at, adding: 'it's never a red card.' Liverpool's head coach, Andoni Iraola, said it was a clear foul and a red card. Video assistant referee John Brooks did not recommend a review, because the officials did not see a clear and obvious error. Former players on television, including Gary Neville, agreed with the sending-off.

The team selection drew attention before kick-off. Pochettino chose Matthijs de Ligt, who was making his first Premier League start in 12 months after a back injury, and Yoro ahead of Harry Maguire. One match report said De Ligt was at fault for three of the goals. Maguire came on, and United switched to a five-man defence.

United have now conceded 30 goals in 15 games this season, and their defensive record is the worst in Europe's top flight, according to one analysis. Their last defeat at Anfield was a 7-0 loss. Pochettino has time to work on the side, but United are expected to show improvement soon. Yoro is available for the Champions League match at Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday, where Pochettino must decide whether to pick De Ligt and Yoro together again. United's next league game is against Brentford at Old Trafford next Saturday.
